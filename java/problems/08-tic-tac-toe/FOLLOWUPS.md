# Follow-ups — 08 Tic-Tac-Toe

Open **one version at a time**, only after the previous one passes. Each is designed to stress the architecture you chose.
Tests for follow-ups are **not provided** unless they fit the existing contract — extend the contract/tests yourself;
deciding how to test it is part of the exercise.

### V2 — Undo
Take back the last N moves (even after a win). What does your move/state representation look like?

### V3 — More than two players
Three or four players with their own marks, still `K` in a row. What was hard-coded to two?

### V4 — Computer opponent
A perfect player for 3×3, a heuristic for larger boards. How does your board representation help?

### V5 — Gravity and other rules
Connect-Four style gravity (marks fall), or a *misère* variant where completing a line loses. Which part of your design absorbs the change?
